Flat forgot the handle
№ 2 of 6 · 2 min read

Nielsen Norman Group tracked what a decade of ultra-flat design did to users: they hesitate before every click.

Interfaces that stripped away clickability cues made users measurably slower - they hovered and tapped experimentally across pages because nothing told them what was interactive. Removing decoration is fine. Removing information is not.

Why it matters

Polish that hides the button taxes every single viewer, silently.

Filed from Nielsen Norman Group ↗
